Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rifat Media Manager (“we”, “our”, or “the Service”) handles information when you use our social media management dashboard and connect social media accounts, including TikTok.

Important: Rifat Media Manager is a management tool. You choose whether to connect an account and authorize the permissions requested by the relevant platform.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the Service may process:

  • Basic account and profile details made available by a platform, such as platform user ID, username, display name, and profile image.
  • Authorization information, such as access tokens and token expiry details, needed to connect your account. These credentials should be stored securely and are not intended to be displayed publicly.
  • Content and publishing details you provide, such as video files, captions, titles, scheduled times, and publishing status.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service, such as logs and error details.

2. How we use information

We use information only as needed to provide and maintain the Service, connect accounts you authorize, prepare or publish content at your direction, show publishing results, protect the Service, and respond to support requests.

3. Platform connections and sharing

When you connect a third-party platform, that platform may receive information or content when you use an integration feature. Your use of TikTok and other platforms is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Storage, security, and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Service, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with applicable obligations. Access tokens should be protected with restricted access and removed when an account is disconnected or authorization is no longer needed.

5. Your choices and rights

You can choose not to connect an account, revoke an integration from the relevant platform, or stop using the Service. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your use of the Service, subject to applicable requirements and technical limitations.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for children under the minimum age required by the applicable social media platform or local law.
